# Roadmap

The roadmap tracks concrete product work rather than delivery dates.

- Expand generated-ground-truth evaluation to all supported deterministic and bounded semantic finding families.
- Add adapter implementations for optional local retrieval only after quality measurements justify them.
- Surface policy coverage and retrieval metrics in focused local views when their report contracts are stable.
- Add broader reproducible local-model benchmarks across documented hardware profiles.
- Validate the simple AI-guided review flow in Obsidian desktop, then promote
  it only after its manual acceptance gate passes.
- Prepare manual packaging, compatibility, accessibility, and security evidence required for wider distribution.

The roadmap is not a promise of release order or availability.
