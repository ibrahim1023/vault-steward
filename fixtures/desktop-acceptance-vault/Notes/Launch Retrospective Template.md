# Launch Retrospective Template

## What happened

Describe the sequence of events from cohort invitation through the first
successful validation. Separate observed facts from interpretations.

## What helped

Capture decisions, documentation, and collaboration patterns that reduced
uncertainty for administrators or the delivery team.

## What should change

Describe the smallest next experiment. Link to a decision record if the change
alters launch scope, risk tolerance, or the supported customer workflow.
