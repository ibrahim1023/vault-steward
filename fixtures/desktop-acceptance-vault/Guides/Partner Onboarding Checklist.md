# Partner Onboarding Checklist

## Before the session

1. Confirm that the partner manager has read the Northstar product brief.
2. Share the pilot boundaries and explain that the first release is intentionally
   limited to guided workspace setup.
3. Verify that the partner understands how to route support requests rather than
   promising direct configuration work.

## During the session

Use a fictional workspace and make the role-template trade-offs visible. Ask
the partner to explain why validation precedes invitations. The goal is not to
memorize a script; it is to help a customer make a reversible first decision.

## After the session

Capture unresolved questions in the decision log. Do not add customer details
to this training note.
