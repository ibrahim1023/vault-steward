# Northstar Product Brief

## Problem

New administrators currently receive a spreadsheet, a recorded meeting, and a
support contact. Teams with a clear internal owner can work through that
process, but smaller teams often stall before they have invited anyone. The
result is a slow, inconsistent start and a support queue full of basic setup
questions.

## Proposed experience

Northstar replaces the spreadsheet with a guided setup. It asks for a workspace
name, suggests a role template, imports a small starter configuration, and
shows a validation report before the administrator invites collaborators.

## Success measures

1. At least 70% of pilot teams finish the guided setup without a support call.
2. Median time from account creation to first validation is under 15 minutes.
3. Fewer than 10% of pilot teams request a full configuration reset in week one.

## Non-goals

The launch is not a migration product. It will not attempt to reconcile legacy
permissions, replace enterprise identity configuration, or automate billing
changes. Those workflows have different risks and need separate discovery.

## Open questions

We still need to decide whether the invitation screen should appear before or
after the validation report. Pilot feedback strongly favors validation first,
but the current copy is too technical for non-specialist administrators.
