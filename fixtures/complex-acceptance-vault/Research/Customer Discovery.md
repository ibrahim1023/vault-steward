# Customer Discovery

Regional administrators consistently asked for a way to inspect a proposed
role import before the system changed a customer workspace. They considered
clear rollback ownership more valuable than automatic correction.

## Evidence summary

Eight of twelve interviewees had previously waited for central operations to
fix a missing role mapping. Five described an escalation handoff that lost the
original import context.
