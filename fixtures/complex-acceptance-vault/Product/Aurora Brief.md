# Aurora Brief

Atlas solves a recurring onboarding problem: regional administrators need to
import workforce roles without asking central operations to edit every mapping.
The first cohort begins on **2026-09-02** after the August security review.

## Non-goals

The release does not include external identity-provider provisioning, bulk
rollback of historical imports, or autonomous changes to production roles.

## Evidence

Customer interviews show that operators want a clear preview before applying a
role import. The supporting research is in [[Research/Customer Discovery]].
